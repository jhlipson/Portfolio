Feel No Evil – Vertical Slice (2026)

Role: Technical Director

  • Character movement systems
  • Modular weapon architecture
  • Player statistics framework
  • UI architecture
  • Inventory systems
  • Role-playing mechanics integration
  • Lead programming team on general architecture

Working with a team of 10 individuals, Feel No Evil was the biggest project I have worked on thus far. The following is a rough prototype from two months of consitent development. The project in question is a horror title focused on the player facing down creatures stronger than themselves only to become more powerful the more they face their fears. Use the axe to fight back, with each kill the player's level increases, and their weapon's individual power, independent of the player increases.

As Technical Director for this title my role was to delegate and assign programming tasks to other programmers, design the overall archictecture of the game, communicate between channels, handle plugin libraries like LeanTween, and create systems from scratch such as the UI functionality for Feel No Evil.

I designed a modular weapon system that allowed new weapon types to be added seamlessly as the project evolved. I built the axe combat flow, implemented player statistics, UI, inventory system, additive scene loading manager, and ensured tight integration between combat systems and the game's role-playing mechanics.

A controls section is included in this build of the game. The vertical slice proved to be a substantial improvement from the prototype version six months prior.
